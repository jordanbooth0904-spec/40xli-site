About

Coming from the land up north, Matt calls Canada home. Don't let his iRating fool you: Matt is a sneaky-fast racer with an enormous amount of league racing knowledge. He holds plenty of trophies and championships, with a tremendous skill for saving his tires and finding a way to the front on restarts. Matt also jumps into the broadcast booth occasionally, but secretly, all he wants to do is race.
